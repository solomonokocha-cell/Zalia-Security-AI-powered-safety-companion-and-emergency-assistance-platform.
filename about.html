Emergency Operational Guidelines

Recommended steps for personal security when navigating Rivers State routes:

  • 1

    Use the Emergency Dispatch Hub: In an active emergency, open the Emergency Contacts page to tap the red Call 112 button or send a pre-filled WhatsApp SOS message containing your location.

  • 2

    Map Routes with Satellite Navigation: Open the Route Planner to view high-definition satellite imagery, verify turn-by-turn directions, and synthesize speech guidance before entering remote transit corridors.

  • 3

    Consult Zazi AI Chat Prior to Travel: Visit the Zazi AI page to ask her about traffic alerts, weather updates, and route-specific safety advice before embarking on your journey.

  • 4

    Log Incident Reports: If you observe any suspicious activity, road blockades, or hazards, report it on the Incidents page so other community members are informed in real-time.

  • 5

    Practice the Weather Disguise Mode: Test switching into weather camouflage mode so you can quickly mask your screen if stopped or monitored in public.
